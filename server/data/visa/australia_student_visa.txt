Australia Student Visa (Subclass 500)

International students who want to study in Australia must apply for a Student Visa (Subclass 500). This visa covers full-time enrolment at an Australian university, TAFE, school, or registered training organisation.

Required Documents:
- Valid passport
- Confirmation of Enrolment (CoE) from your Australian education provider — issued after you accept your offer and pay any required upfront fees
- Genuine Student requirement: a statement explaining why you want to study in Australia, your ties to your home country, and your plans after study. This has replaced the older Genuine Temporary Entrant (GTE) requirement from 2024
- Proof of financial capacity: sufficient funds to cover tuition fees, living costs (approximately 24,505 AUD per year as of 2024), and return travel
- English language proficiency: IELTS, TOEFL, PTE Academic, or Cambridge results meeting your institution's requirements
- Overseas Student Health Cover (OSHC): mandatory health insurance for the duration of your visa, purchased from an approved Australian provider before applying. Major providers: Medibank, Bupa, Allianz Care, AHM, NIB
- Academic transcripts and qualifications
- If under 18: additional requirements including welfare arrangements and guardian information

Genuine Student Requirement:
From March 2024, the Department of Home Affairs replaced the GTE statement with the Genuine Student (GS) requirement. You must demonstrate you are a genuine student intending to comply with visa conditions, that study in Australia aligns with your circumstances and aspirations, and that you intend to return home after your studies.

Processing Time:
Apply online through ImmiAccount at immi.homeaffairs.gov.au. Processing time is typically 4 to 6 weeks but can vary significantly. Apply at least 2 to 3 months before your intended travel date. Most students can apply up to 12 months before their course start date.

Overseas Student Health Cover (OSHC):
OSHC is compulsory — you must purchase it before applying for your visa and maintain it for the entire duration of your stay. It covers visits to the doctor, some hospital treatment, and limited pharmaceuticals. It does not cover dental, optical, or physiotherapy. Cost is approximately 600 to 700 AUD per year for a single student.

After Arrival:
Students are typically granted a multiple-entry visa valid for the duration of their course plus an additional period. You must maintain full-time enrolment, satisfactory academic progress, and approved health cover. Register with your university's international student services office. Obtain a Tax File Number (TFN) from the Australian Taxation Office if you plan to work.

Working Rights:
Student Visa (Subclass 500) holders can work up to 48 hours per fortnight (two weeks) during term time. There is no limit on work hours during official course breaks. This increased from the previous 40-hour fortnightly limit in 2023 — check the Department of Home Affairs website for the most current conditions.

After Graduation:
Apply for a Temporary Graduate Visa (Subclass 485) after completing your studies to work in Australia. The length of the visa depends on your qualification and the location where you studied. Students who studied in regional Australia may be eligible for longer post-study work rights.

Living Costs:
The Australian government requires you to demonstrate approximately 24,505 AUD per year for living costs. In reality, budget for: rent 800 to 1,500 AUD per month (varies hugely by city), groceries 200 to 400 AUD per month, transport 100 to 200 AUD per month. Sydney and Melbourne are the most expensive cities; Adelaide, Brisbane, and Perth are more affordable.
