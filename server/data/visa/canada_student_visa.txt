Canada Student Visa (Study Permit)

International students who want to study in Canada for more than 6 months must obtain a Study Permit. This is not a visa itself but an official document that allows you to study at a Designated Learning Institution (DLI) in Canada.

Required Documents:
- Valid passport
- Letter of Acceptance from a Designated Learning Institution (DLI) — your university must be on the DLI list
- Proof of financial support: you must show you can cover tuition fees plus living costs of at least 10,000 CAD per year (or 11,000 CAD if studying in Quebec), plus return transportation
- Completed application forms (IMM 1294 for Study Permit)
- Digital photograph meeting IRCC specifications
- Statement of purpose: a letter explaining why you want to study in Canada, why you chose your programme and institution, and your plans after graduation
- Academic transcripts and certificates
- Language test results (IELTS, TOEFL, or French equivalent if applicable)
- Biometrics (fingerprints and photo) — required for most nationalities; give biometrics at a visa application centre
- If applicable: provincial attestation letter (PAL) — required for most provinces since January 2024 for new undergraduate applicants due to study permit cap

Provincial Attestation Letter (PAL):
Since January 2024, most international students applying for undergraduate programmes must obtain a Provincial Attestation Letter (PAL) from the province or territory where they plan to study before applying for a Study Permit. Graduate students and some other categories are exempt. Check the IRCC website for current requirements as this policy is evolving.

Processing Time:
Processing times vary by country of application. Check current processing times at ircc.canada.ca. Apply as early as possible — at least 3 to 4 months before your programme start date. You can apply online through the IRCC portal.

Student Direct Stream (SDS):
Students from certain countries (including India, China, Philippines, Vietnam, Senegal, Pakistan, Morocco, and others) may be eligible for the Student Direct Stream, which offers faster processing (typically 20 calendar days) if they meet additional requirements including a GIC (Guaranteed Investment Certificate) of 10,000 CAD and an IELTS score of 6.0 or higher in each band.

Guaranteed Investment Certificate (GIC):
A GIC from a participating Canadian financial institution (such as Scotiabank or CIBC) is a way to demonstrate financial support. You deposit funds which are then released to you in monthly instalments after arrival. Required for SDS applicants; optional but accepted for regular applicants.

After Arrival:
Your study permit will be issued with conditions — read them carefully. You must study at the DLI named on your permit. If you change institutions, you must apply to amend your study permit. Register with your university's international student centre. Apply for a Social Insurance Number (SIN) if you plan to work — you can do this at a Service Canada office with your study permit and passport.

Working Rights:
Study permit holders can work on-campus without a separate work permit. Off-campus work is permitted up to 24 hours per week during term (increased from 20 hours in 2024 — check IRCC for current rules) and full-time during scheduled breaks. After graduation, apply for a Post-Graduation Work Permit (PGWP) — eligible for up to 3 years depending on the length of your programme.

Health Insurance:
Health coverage for international students varies by province. Some provinces include international students in their provincial health plan after a waiting period; others do not. Most universities offer or require a supplementary health insurance plan. Check what is covered in your province before arrival.

Quebec:
Students studying in Quebec have additional requirements. The Quebec Acceptance Certificate (CAQ) from the Ministère de l'Immigration, de la Francisation et de l'Intégration (MIFI) is required before applying for a federal Study Permit. Apply for the CAQ first, then use it in your Study Permit application.
