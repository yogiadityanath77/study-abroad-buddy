France Student Visa (Visa Etudiant)

Non-EU students who want to study in France for more than 90 days must apply for a long-stay student visa (Visa de long séjour étudiant, VLS-TS). EU citizens do not need a visa.

Required Documents:
- Valid passport (valid for at least 3 months beyond the end of your stay)
- Completed visa application form (via Campus France or the French embassy website)
- Recent passport photographs (35mm x 45mm)
- Acceptance letter (lettre d'admission) from a French educational institution
- Campus France registration certificate (for students from countries where the Campus France procedure is compulsory, including India, China, USA, and many others)
- Proof of financial resources: minimum 615 EUR per month (approximately 7,380 EUR per year). Accepted evidence includes bank statements, scholarship letter, or a financial guarantee from family with supporting documents
- Proof of accommodation in France (university residence confirmation, rental agreement, or host family certificate)
- Health insurance covering the duration of your stay in France
- Academic records and transcripts
- Proof of French language proficiency (DELF, DALF, TCF) for French-taught programmes, or English proficiency for English-taught programmes

Campus France Procedure:
Many countries require students to complete the Campus France procedure before applying for a visa. This involves creating a profile on the Campus France website, uploading your documents, and having an interview at the Campus France office in your country. Once validated, you receive a Campus France number to include in your visa application.

Processing Time:
Apply at least 2 to 3 months before your departure. Processing time is typically 2 to 4 weeks after submitting a complete application.

After Arrival:
Within 3 months of arrival, validate your VLS-TS visa online at the ANEF portal (administration-etrangers-en-france.interieur.gouv.fr). This is mandatory — failure to validate means your visa is invalid. Pay the tax stamp (timbre fiscal) of approximately 60 EUR during validation. Register with your university's student services. Apply for French social security and student health insurance (LMDE or SMENO).

Financial Aid:
International students may be eligible for CAF housing benefits (Aide Personnalisée au Logement or APL) which can reduce rent significantly. Apply through the CAF website after arrival.

Working Rights:
Student visa holders in France can work up to 964 hours per year (approximately 20 hours per week) without a separate work permit.
