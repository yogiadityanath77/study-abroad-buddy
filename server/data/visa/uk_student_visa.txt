UK Student Visa

Non-UK and non-Irish nationals who want to study in the United Kingdom must apply for a Student Visa (formerly Tier 4). This applies to full-time courses at a licensed student sponsor institution lasting more than 6 months.

Required Documents:
- Valid passport or travel document
- Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies (CAS) reference number from your university (the university sends this after you accept an offer)
- Proof of financial funds: enough money to cover your course fees plus living costs (at least 1,334 GBP per month for studies in London, or 1,023 GBP per month outside London, for up to 9 months)
- Academic qualifications and transcripts
- English language test results (IELTS for UKVI, minimum band score varies by course level — typically 5.5 to 7.0 overall)
- Tuberculosis test results (required for applicants from certain countries)
- ATAS certificate (Academic Technology Approval Scheme) if your course involves certain sensitive subjects
- Parental consent letter if you are under 18

Processing Time:
Apply online up to 6 months before your course starts. Processing typically takes 3 weeks from outside the UK. You may be asked to provide biometrics at a visa application centre.

Financial Requirements:
You must show you have held the required funds for at least 28 consecutive days before applying. The 28-day period must end no more than 31 days before your application date. Bank statements, building society letters, or official financial sponsorship letters are accepted.

Healthcare Surcharge:
You must pay the Immigration Health Surcharge (IHS) as part of your visa application. This gives you access to the National Health Service (NHS) in the UK. The current rate is 776 GBP per year of study.

After Arrival:
If your visa is a vignette sticker valid for 30 days, collect your Biometric Residence Permit (BRP) from the Post Office branch named in your visa within 10 days of arriving. Register with a local GP (doctor) for NHS access. Register your address with your university.

Working Rights:
Student visa holders can work up to 20 hours per week during term time and full time during official vacations. You cannot be self-employed or work as a professional sportsperson or entertainer.

Application:
Apply online at gov.uk/student-visa. Pay the visa fee (approximately 490 GBP from outside the UK) and the healthcare surcharge online.
